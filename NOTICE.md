# Janus Notice

## AI-Assisted Development

Janus was developed with the assistance of an agentic AI coding assistant.
While PRs and contributions generated or assisted by AI are welcome, human reviewers maintain the ultimate responsibility for verifying the security, correctness, and architectural consistency of submitted code.

Please ensure that AI-generated contributions:
- Align with the project's security-first philosophy (e.g. strict adherence to capability models).
- Properly handle `unsafe` blocks with clear, documented safety invariants.
- Keep complexity minimal where appropriate, especially within the core microkernel execution paths.
